# Stage 09.007 - Resume Adaptation Design

## Purpose

Materialize a factual resume source for VAC-09-004-001 before producing any candidate-facing resume.

The resume itself is not a source of truth. Claims must descend from verified Stage 09 evidence.

## Target

- Vacancy: VAC-09-004-001
- Priority: P2
- Requirements: 8
- Full coverage: 5
- Partial coverage: 2
- None: 0
- Unknown: 1

## Claim policy

- full: direct factual wording allowed only inside verified scope
- partial: qualified wording only
- none: omit
- unknown: do not claim

## Hard boundaries

- Do not claim Advanced English.
- Do not claim Redux, Flux or Relay from React Context.
- Do not claim measured client-side performance.
- Do not infer seniority.
- Do not infer professional duration from repository history.
- Do not import VAC-09-004-002 requirements.
- Do not manufacture ATS percentages or keyword-stuff.

## Execution sequence

1. Freeze factual resume source.
2. Inspect verified evidence available for each permitted claim.
3. Produce candidate-facing resume draft.
4. Trace every material claim to evidence.
5. Run factual and privacy review.
6. Only after human approval may later application gates be considered.

No pre-existing materialized personal resume baseline was discovered.
This task therefore establishes a new evidence-backed factual source before drafting.
