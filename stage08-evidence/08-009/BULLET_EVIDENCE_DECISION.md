## Data Classification Rule

The task and any complete CV remain confidential by default.

A separately sanitized evidence artifact may be classified as repository-safe only when it contains no private contact data, no unauthorized PII and no unsupported career claims.

Repository-safe does not mean externally published or approved for job applications.

## Channel Rule

Tone, ordering and level of detail may vary by career channel. Factual substance, scope, authorship, technologies, results and limitations may not be upgraded beyond accepted evidence.
